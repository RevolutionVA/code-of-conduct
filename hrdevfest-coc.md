# Hampton Roads DevFest Code of Conduct

All attendees, speakers, sponsors, and volunteers at Hampton Roads DevFest are required to agree with the following code of conduct. Organizers will enforce this code throughout the event. We expect cooperation from all participants to help ensure a safe environment for everybody.

## Need Help?

If you need to report an incident or have any concerns, contact the organizing team at [team@revolutionva.org](mailto:team@revolutionva.org). You can also speak directly with any staff member during the event.

## The Quick Version

Hampton Roads DevFest is dedicated to providing a harassment-free conference experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, religion or lack thereof, socioeconomic status, immigration or citizenship status, pregnancy, veteran status, neurodiversity, or technology choices. We do not tolerate harassment of conference participants in any form. Sexual language and imagery are not appropriate for any conference venue, including talks, workshops, parties, social media (including Twitter/X, Discord, and Slack), and other online media. Conference participants violating these rules may be sanctioned or expelled from the conference *without a refund* at the discretion of the conference organizers.

## The Less Quick Version

Harassment includes offensive verbal comments related to gender, gender identity and expression, age, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, religion, technology choices; sexual images in public spaces; deliberate intimidation; stalking; following; harassing photography or recording; sustained disruption of talks or other events; inappropriate physical contact; unwelcome sexual attention; doxing or sharing private information without consent; deliberate misgendering or deadnaming; and threats of violence.

Participants asked to stop any harassing behavior are expected to comply immediately.

Sponsors are also subject to the anti-harassment policy. In particular, sponsors should not use sexualized images, activities, or other material. Booth staff, including volunteers, should not use sexualized clothing, uniforms, or costumes, or otherwise create a sexualized environment.

## Consequences

If a participant engages in harassing behavior, the conference organizers may take action including but not limited to:

1. A verbal warning and request to stop the behavior
2. Temporary removal from the event space
3. Permanent expulsion from the conference with no refund
4. Referral to venue security or local law enforcement

The severity of the response will be determined by the organizers based on the nature and context of the behavior.

## Reporting Process

If you are being harassed, notice that someone else is being harassed, or have any other concerns, contact a member of the staff immediately. Hampton Roads DevFest staff can be identified by their designated staff T-shirts and badges.

You can also report incidents by emailing [team@revolutionva.org](mailto:team@revolutionva.org). When you make a report, here is what you can expect:

- **Acknowledgment:** Your report will be acknowledged promptly.
- **Confidentiality:** All reports will be handled with discretion. Details will only be shared as needed to investigate and resolve the situation.
- **Investigation:** The organizers will review the report and speak with involved parties as appropriate.
- **Outcome:** You will be informed of the resolution. Actions taken will follow the consequences outlined above.

Hampton Roads DevFest staff will be happy to help participants contact venue or event staff or local law enforcement, or otherwise assist those experiencing harassment to feel safe for the duration of the conference. We value your attendance.

## Photography and Recording

We want everyone to feel comfortable at Hampton Roads DevFest. Please ask for consent before photographing or recording other attendees. If someone asks you not to photograph or record them, respect their wishes. Official event photographers will be clearly identified; if you prefer not to be photographed, let them know.

## Applicability

We expect participants to follow these rules at all Hampton Roads DevFest venues, conference-related social events, and event-specific online platforms such as Discord, Slack, or other community channels. This code of conduct also applies to interactions between participants after the event if they stem from conduct that occurred during the conference. Continued harassment outside the event will be treated with the same seriousness.

## Accessibility

Hampton Roads DevFest is committed to providing an accessible and inclusive environment for all participants. If you need accommodations to fully participate in the event, contact us at [team@revolutionva.org](mailto:team@revolutionva.org), and we will do our best to meet your needs.
