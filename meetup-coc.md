<h1>RevolutionVA Meetup Code of Conduct</h1>

<p>RevolutionVA is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone. This code of conduct applies to all meetup participants, including attendees, speakers, organizers, volunteers, sponsors, venue staff, and guests. By participating in a RevolutionVA meetup or community space, you agree to follow this code.</p>

<h2>Need Help?</h2>

<p>If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other safety concern, contact a meetup organizer in person or email <a href="mailto:team@revolutionva.org">team@revolutionva.org</a>. You may report something during or after an event.</p>

<p>If anyone is in immediate danger, contact venue security or local emergency services first. Organizers can help you do so.</p>

<h2>Expected Behavior</h2>

<p>We expect participants to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Treat others with respect, empathy, and consideration.</li>
<li>Welcome people of all backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and technology choices.</li>
<li>Respect personal boundaries, names, pronouns, privacy, and requests to stop an unwelcome behavior.</li>
<li>Keep discussions constructive and allow others a reasonable opportunity to participate.</li>
<li>Ask for consent before photographing or recording an identifiable person.</li>
<li>Follow venue rules and organizer instructions related to safety and accessibility.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Unacceptable Behavior</h2>

<p>Harassment and other unacceptable behavior include, but are not limited to:</p>
<ul>
<li>Offensive or discriminatory comments or conduct related to gender, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion or lack of religion, age, socioeconomic status, immigration or citizenship status, pregnancy, veteran status, neurodiversity, or technology choices.</li>
<li>Sexual language or imagery in event spaces, presentations, demonstrations, social activities, or community channels.</li>
<li>Unwelcome sexual attention, inappropriate physical contact, or repeated unwanted personal attention.</li>
<li>Deliberate intimidation, stalking, following, threats, or incitement of violence.</li>
<li>Harassing photography or recording, or continuing to photograph or record someone after being asked to stop.</li>
<li>Doxing or sharing another person's private information without permission.</li>
<li>Deliberate misgendering or use of a name someone has asked you not to use.</li>
<li>Sustained disruption of talks, activities, discussions, or online community spaces.</li>
<li>Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern, makes a report, or participates in an investigation.</li>
</ul>

<p>Reasonable, respectful disagreement and good-faith technical critique are not harassment. However, participants asked to stop an unacceptable behavior are expected to comply immediately.</p>

<h2>Scope</h2>

<p>This code applies at all RevolutionVA meetup activities, including talks, workshops, networking sessions, meals, social gatherings, and event-related travel. It also applies in meetup-related online spaces and to one-on-one communications that arise from participation in the community.</p>

<p>Behavior outside an official event may be considered when it creates a safety concern for participants or materially affects someone's ability to participate in the community.</p>

<h2>Reporting and Response</h2>

<p>Reports may be made by the person affected or by a witness. When possible, include what happened, when and where it occurred, who was involved, and whether there is an immediate safety concern. You are not required to confront the person whose behavior you are reporting.</p>

<p>Organizers will:</p>
<ul>
<li>Acknowledge reports as promptly as practical.</li>
<li>Listen without requiring a reporter to prove harm before receiving support.</li>
<li>Share information only with people who need it to assess and respond to the situation, except where disclosure is required by law or necessary to address an immediate safety risk.</li>
<li>Avoid assigning a report to an organizer who is involved in the complaint or has a significant conflict of interest.</li>
<li>Consider the reporter's safety and preferences while retaining discretion to protect the broader community.</li>
<li>Document and review the situation sufficiently to determine an appropriate response.</li>
</ul>

<p>We cannot promise complete confidentiality or a particular outcome, but we will handle reports with care, discretion, and fairness. Knowingly false reports made in bad faith may themselves violate this code; a report that cannot be substantiated is not, by itself, a false report.</p>

<h2>Enforcement</h2>

<p>Organizers may take any action reasonably necessary to maintain a safe and welcoming environment. Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:</p>
<ol>
<li>A private conversation, warning, or request to stop the behavior</li>
<li>Removal from a discussion, activity, online space, or event</li>
<li>Restrictions on future participation</li>
<li>A temporary or permanent ban from RevolutionVA events and community spaces</li>
<li>Notification of venue staff, another community's organizers, an employer or sponsor when directly relevant, or law enforcement when appropriate</li>
</ol>

<p>Responses will be based on the nature, severity, context, and pattern of behavior. Organizers may act without first issuing a warning when necessary for safety or when the conduct is severe.</p>

<h2>Speakers, Sponsors, and Organizers</h2>

<p>Presentation materials, demonstrations, sponsor activities, and promotional content are subject to this code. Sexualized or discriminatory content and environments are not appropriate unless the material is necessary to the topic, is presented professionally, and has been discussed with the organizers in advance.</p>

<p>Organizers and community leaders are held to the same standards as every other participant. Reports concerning an organizer should be directed to another organizer or to <a href="mailto:team@revolutionva.org">team@revolutionva.org</a> so that someone without a conflict can handle the report.</p>

<h2>Accessibility</h2>

<p>We want our meetups to be accessible and inclusive. Contact <a href="mailto:team@revolutionva.org">team@revolutionva.org</a> about accommodations or barriers to participation, and we will make a good-faith effort to help.</p>
